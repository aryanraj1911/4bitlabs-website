About us

A small team with a big soldering iron

4BitLabs started in Nagpur in 2025 with one idea: students should learn technology by building it, not just reading about it.

Our story

Building the future of tech education

Founded by Aman Patel, 4BitLabs is a Nagpur startup on a mission to make technology education hands-on.

We do three things: we manufacture robotics kits and learning tools, we run workshops in schools and colleges, and we host gamified tech events like hackathons, quizzes and e-sports competitions.
